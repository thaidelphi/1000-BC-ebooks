# Chapter 14: The Mala Chemical Deterrent and the Radial Armor

"Eight men... We have barely eight spearmen fit to bear weapons!"

Kuran's hoarse, strained whisper cracked like dry timber against the tense silence surrounding the clay Rocket Stove hearth inside the winter longhouse.

The terrifying threats of War Chief Kraak's hundred spears and the modern "Thunder God Victor"—bellowed by the bound scouts confined in the rock alcove—echoed in the back of everyone's mind like an approaching death sentence.

The pale morning light filtering across the ten-wheeler's steel cab and the pure water trickling through the "Green Dragon Bamboo Aqueduct" did nothing to dispel the suffocating dread choking the settlement.

Kuran, Shaman Moka, Elya, and Thame sat gathered in an unbroken circle of grim, desperate consultation.

The smokeless radiant warmth took the edge off the morning freeze, but could not dispel the profound dread etched across the veteran chieftain's face.

Kuran sketched an attack diagram into the hard-packed dirt floor with a twig, gesturing with short, mutually understood words in a hoarse, strained whisper:

"Clan Cliff Deer has only eight spearmen fit to bear weapons... Count me, Elya, and you, we stand barely eleven defenders in all."

"If one hundred cannibals charge the gorge... No matter how sturdy the palisade, their human wave will climb over their own dead and butcher us all."

Shaman Moka added with trembling dread, pointing a withered finger toward the towering cliffs: "The women, children, and elders must retreat into the Secret Cliff Sanctuary."

Elya sat silently honing the edges of her flint arrowheads with the diamond sharpening stone, the obsidian tips flashing in the firelight as her dark eyes burned with deadly resolve.

Thame nodded with steady, calculated composure. In the annals of military history, a ten-to-one numerical disadvantage was sheer suicide in any direct frontal clash.

"If we match brute force with brute force, we are completely doomed," Thame reasoned methodically to himself.

"We must fight an asymmetric war, wielding twenty-first-century equipment that these stone-age warriors cannot even comprehend."

Thame stood up and signaled Kuran to follow him directly to the rear of the cargo container.

The first critical priority was absolute food security; once under siege, hunting parties would be unable to leave the sanctuary of the perimeter.

Thame retrieved a heavy corrugated carton marked with tracking code `#TH-631029`, weighing 11.5 kilograms, and placed it upon the workbench.

The shipping waybill indicated a bulk order from a renowned wholesale meat-curing and food preservation depot in northern Thailand.

Slicing through the reinforced packing tape with his pocket knife, the contents were revealed in pristine condition:

**A 10-kilogram sack of coarse pure sea salt, 50 vacuum-seal food preservation pouches, and 500 grams of coarse cracked black pepper.**

Thame handed the salt and black pepper to Kuran, instructing the clan's women to construct a specialized "limestone smokehouse" behind the longhouses.

Over sixty kilograms of dire wolf and Irish elk venison were sliced into thin, uniform strips and thoroughly coated with pure sea salt and black pepper.

The white salt crystals rapidly extracted intracellular moisture, inhibiting bacterial spoilage while replenishing vital sodium chloride to ward off muscle cramps among the warriors.

Under ordinary conditions, prehistoric hunters received scant sodium from raw animal blood; pure marine salt provided a massive restorative surge to their physical endurance.

Fragrant smoke from dried pine twigs and oak bark cured the meat to dry perfection, filling the crisp morning air with a savory aroma—an enduring war vault had been secured.

Next, Thame confronted their deadliest tactical dilemma: stopping a hundred-man human wave at the four-meter chokepoint.

He knew all too well that his precious Smith & Wesson .38 revolver ammunition, conserved strictly at 43 rounds, could not be squandered recklessly.

Thame scanned deep into the dim cargo container, where thousands of assorted parcels were stacked haphazardly from floor to ceiling. Clambering inside, he spent several minutes playing his headlamp over scuffed shipping labels.

His eyes locked onto a stout corrugated carton labeled `#TH-901238`, weighing 5.2 kilograms, dispatched from a famous Sichuan Mala hotpot franchise destined for an upscale mountain resort. Slitting the tape open, he revealed **five massive bags of finely ground Sichuan Mala chili powder blended with concentrated habanero pepper flakes, totaling 5 kilograms**. The sharp, pungent aroma stung his nostrils immediately.

Contemplating how to propel the chili suspension, Thame climbed over another stack of crates to search an opposite corner, sweeping his beam across taped labels and merchant barcodes until he uncovered parcel `#TH-440182`, weighing 1.8 kilograms from a seasonal festival toy vendor. Inside rested **two heavy-duty pump-action Super Soaker water guns** with 2.5-liter pressurized reservoirs, molded from rugged, neon-orange and vibrant blue impact-resistant plastic.

The two items hailed from entirely different vendors, served completely unrelated purposes, and had been crammed into separate corners of the trailer. Yet in the young engineer's analytical mind, the synthesis flashed like lightning.

"This is literally... a crowd-control riot chemical cannon, hotpot style!" Thame let out a hearty laugh of immense relief.

The ultra-concentrated capsaicin combined with the neuro-active hydroxy-alpha-sanshool of Sichuan peppercorns represented a devastating, non-lethal sensory disruptor.

Using a piece of clean gauze cloth to strain out coarse chili flakes and prevent nozzle clogs, Thame dissolved the fiery crimson powder into warm water drawn from the bamboo aqueduct until it formed an opaque, scalding-red chemical slurry. A pungent, sinus-stinging haze instantly billowed forth, forcing him to tie a cloth mask across his face.

He poured the liquid into the twin reservoirs of the water blasters, sealed the threaded caps tight, and vigorously pumped the dual-action charging handles until the internal air chambers tightened to maximum pressure.

"Kuran, Elya, come watch this carefully!" Thame summoned them both to the timber gate for a live demonstration.

Shouldering the neon-orange blaster like a tactical rifle, Thame aimed out through the gate gap at a dead pine stump standing nearly ten meters beyond the palisade, then pulled the trigger!

*Fsssshh—SPLAASH!*

A thick, high-velocity stream of boiling-red liquid tore through the cold air in a tight ballistic arc, slamming squarely into the dead stump nearly ten meters away with a wet, heavy crack! The wood was instantly drenched in foaming orange heat, and the harsh mountain winds failed to deflect the dense, pressurized water jet in the slightest.

"Incredible... A range of nearly ten meters, far outstripping any thrusting spear!" Thame shouted in triumphant satisfaction.

Kuran stood frozen, his jaw slack. As the biting stench of atomized peppercorns drifted back from the stump, he sneezed loudly, staring at the brightly colored orange weapon in Thame's hands with wide-eyed dread.

"A fire-spewing water tube... No bow, no spear, yet it hurls burning venom that far!" Kuran gasped in a trembling voice, convinced Thame had summoned an enchanted fire-beast.

Little Tara giggled at the toy's vivid neon colors, while Elya, perched on the rocky rise, observed with razor-sharp tactical comprehension.

She saw clearly that this "red water" flew far beyond the throwing reach of primitive javelins, capable of blinding enemy shock troops before they could ever reach the barricade.

"Orok... Cannot reach us... Eyes blinded!" Elya pointed at the blaster, her eyes blazing with predatory brilliance.

"Exactly, Elya..." Thame grinned with fierce confidence. "At ten meters out, before they can even cock their throwing arms, their vanguard will be clutching their melting eyes and writhing in the dirt!"

The next critical objective was outfitting their frontline defenders with close-combat body armor.

Thame walked over to the shredded rear dual wheels of the ten-wheeler, destroyed during the plunge off the mountain pass.

Modern heavy commercial truck radial tires are constructed from fifteen-millimeter vulcanized rubber reinforced with interwoven high-tensile steel belts.

Engineered to withstand ten tons of axle load and over one hundred PSI of internal pressure, they possessed unmatched puncture resistance against primitive weapons.

Thame wielded the SK5 folding saw alongside his heavy carbon Bowie knife, sawing out curved rectangular tread plates shaped to fit a human chest.

Using the 20V cordless hammer drill, he bored clean perimeter holes, lacing them with 100-pound PE line and 550 paracord.

Within two hours, four sets of "Steel-Belted Radial Chest Cuirasses" and matching forearm vambraces were successfully fabricated.

Kuran stroked the jet-black, ridged rubber cuirass with cautious wonder before strapping it tightly across his muscular chest.

"Try thrusting your flint spear into it with everything you have, Kuran," Thame challenged him.

Kuran gritted his teeth, leveled his sharpest flint-tipped hunting spear, and lunged forward, thrusting the weapon into his own chest armor with full force!

*Thud! Crack!*

The brittle flint tip shattered into shards upon impacting the dense rubber tread and internal steel-wire mesh.

The tire tread bore scarcely a scratch; Kuran felt no shock transmitted through the dense vulcanized barrier.

"Miraculous... Its hide is tougher and more impenetrable than the armor of a giant woolly rhino!" Kuran gasped in awe, his gaze filled with profound reverence.

The Cliff Deer hunters erupted into thunderous cheers, hailing the tire armor as an impenetrable blessing that banished their fear of the hundred spears.

Thame handed the second Super Soaker blaster to Kuran, drilling him on pumping the pressure handle and sighting along the barrel, while placing two buckets of fresh water behind the stone wall for refilling and emergency eye-washing.

For maximum safety against secondary splashback, Thame distributed dampened cotton cloth strips for the frontline hunters to wear as particulate masks and donned clear impact goggles from the toolbox.

Most importantly, to protect the gunners from returning javelins that primitive hunters could cast across ten meters, Thame ordered everyone to stay crouched low behind the heavy limestone barricade, protruding only the plastic muzzle tips through narrow vertical firing loopholes. He assigned rubber-armored warriors to hold timber shields overhead as top cover, instructing Kuran to unleash the scalding red jet stream precisely when the invaders bottlenecked inside the four-meter canyon throat—where the press of bodies would prevent them from cocking their throwing arms—blinding their vanguard before a single spear could take flight!

Moka began escorting little Tara, the women, and the elders up the cliff trail toward the Secret Cliff Sanctuary with water gourds and smoked meat.

Elya strapped on a radial forearm guard, adjusted her composite recurve bow, and checked the quiver of twenty flint arrows slung over her back.

She met Thame's gaze with unwavering, unspoken trust before climbing to the high sentry ledge overlooking the chokepoint.

Thame checked the Smith & Wesson .38 revolver nestled in his chest holster; all 43 rounds remained safely accounted for.

He reaffirmed his ironclad resolve never to pull the trigger unless faced with an absolute, fatal catastrophe.

Thame climbed to the cab roof, refreshing the online war room thread on his flickering 2G phone.

`@PepperSpray_Tactics`: *`Genius idea using high-pressure festival water blasters! The dense jet stream punches ten meters out, completely immune to wind blowback and out-ranging primitive spears. One direct face shot will shatter their vanguard!`*

`@DieselMech_Detroit`: *`Radial tire armor makes stone spears obsolete; commercial truck tires reinforced with steel plies cannot be pierced by paleolithic flint!`*

The eight warriors, outfitted with rubber cuirasses and gripping their spears and axes, took position behind the zigzag barricade in soaring morale.

A tense, absolute silence descended over Fortress Alpha, until the frantic thumping of every defender's heart was audible in the chill air.

*Tweet! Tweet! Tweet!*

Suddenly, three piercing blasts from the titanium whistle echoed down from Elya's sentry perch high atop the limestone cliff!

The triple blast signaled the immediate arrival of the enemy warband!

Thame raised his monocular toward the southern limestone ridges three kilometers away, cutting through the midday sun glare.

Along the crest, savage pelt banners fluttered amidst billowing dust clouds,

And the deep, hollow roar of mammoth-bone war horns reverberated across the canyon—the thirty-man vanguard of the Demon Fang Clan had arrived!
