# Chapter 14: The Mala Chemical Deterrent and the Radial Armor

Early morning mist on the eighth day lingered over the precipitous limestone crags as temperature climbed to 7°C.
Pale late-autumn sunlight struck the glaze of hoarfrost on the ten-wheeler's cab roof, melting it into clear dewdrops.
Pure water from the alpine springhead continued to flow steadily through the "Green Dragon Bamboo Aqueduct" into the central cistern.
Yet the atmosphere inside Fortress Alpha was strained to the breaking point; not a soul dared to laugh or whisper in jest.
The three captured Demon Fang scouts remained bound tightly with 8-strand PE line, confined within a narrow rock alcove under guard.
Their grim threats of War Chief Kraak's hundred spears and the "Thunder God Victor" echoed in the back of everyone's mind.
Kuran, Shaman Moka, Elya, and Thame sat in a circle beside the clay Rocket Stove hearth inside the winter longhouse.
The smokeless warmth took the edge off the morning chill, but could not dispel the deep dread etched across the elder chief's face.
Kuran sketched an attack plan into the hard-packed dirt with a twig, gesturing with short, mutually understood words:
"Clan Cliff Deer has only eight spearmen fit for war... Count me, Elya, and you, we are barely eleven defenders."
"If one hundred cannibals charge the gorge... No matter the timber wall, their human wave will climb over their own dead and butcher us all."
Moka added with trembling dread, pointing toward the high cliffs: "The women, children, and elders must retreat into the Secret Cliff Sanctuary."
Elya sat silently honing the edges of her flint arrowheads with the diamond sharpening stone, her dark eyes burning with fierce determination.
Thame nodded with steady composure. In prehistoric warfare, a ten-to-one numerical disadvantage was sheer suicide in a head-on clash.
"If we fight with brute force alone, we are dead," Thame reasoned internally.
"We must fight an asymmetric war, wielding modern tools and concepts these stone-age warriors have never conceived."
Thame rose and led Kuran back toward the rear of the cargo container.
The first vital priority was food security; once under siege, hunting beyond the perimeter would be impossible.
Thame retrieved a heavy carton marked with tracking code `#TH-631029`, weighing 11.5 kilograms, and severed the tape.
The shipping label indicated an order from a northern wholesale meat-curing and food preservation depot.
Peeling back the corrugated flaps revealed the contents in crisp detail:
**A 10-kilogram sack of coarse sea salt, 50 vacuum-seal food preservation pouches, and 500 grams of coarse cracked black pepper.**
Thame handed the salt and black pepper to Kuran, directing the clan's women to construct a limestone smokehouse behind the living quarters.
Over sixty kilograms of dire wolf and Irish elk venison were sliced into long strips and thoroughly coated with sea salt and pepper.
The white salt crystals drew out moisture, preventing microbial spoilage while replenishing vital sodium chloride to ward off muscle cramps.
Normally, prehistoric hunters received scant minerals from animal blood; pristine sea salt offered a massive restorative surge to their vigor.
Fragrant smoke from pine needles and cured oak bark dried the meat thoroughly, filling the chilly air with a rich, savory aroma—the war vault was stocked.
Next, Thame confronted their deadliest tactical dilemma: breaking a hundred-man human wave at the 4-meter chokepoint.
He could not afford to squander his precious Smith & Wesson .38 revolver ammunition, strictly conserved at 43 rounds.
Scanning the interior cargo stacks, his eyes locked onto a stout corrugated box labeled `#TH-901238`, weighing 6.2 kilograms.
The waybill noted a shipment from a renowned Sichuan Mala hotpot restaurant franchise destined for a mountain resort.
Slitting the tape open, Thame's eyes widened in sheer, astonished delight.
Inside were **five massive bags of finely ground Sichuan Mala chili powder blended with concentrated habanero flakes, totaling 5 kilograms**!
And nested beneath the chili bags were **two 2-liter manual pump pressure sprayers with adjustable brass nozzles**!
"This is literally... weaponized Sichuan hotpot tear gas!" Thame let out a relieved laugh.
Taking the sprayer to the bamboo aqueduct, he filled it with warm water and emptied half a kilogram of the fiery red powder inside.
A pungent, sinus-stinging haze of Sichuan peppercorns and dried chilies instantly filled the air.
Thame shook the canister vigorously until it emulsified into a boiling-red, fiery orange suspension.
He pumped the piston handle until the pressure tank was rock-hard, dialing the brass nozzle to a wide aerosol fan.
"Kuran, Elya, watch this closely!" Thame called them over near the palisade gate for a live demonstration.
Yet the moment Thame pressed the brass trigger to unleash the red mist, a sudden gust of wind whipped back across the gate!
*Whooosh!*
"Cough! Hack! Argh!"
Thame choked violently, his face turning beet-red as tears and mucus poured down his cheeks, blinding heat searing his eyes and nostrils.
Kuran, standing beside him, inhaled merely a fraction of the aerosol plume and sneezed violently a dozen times in a row, stumbling backward in agony.
"Fire! The venomous fire-dust of the mountain spirits!" Kuran cried out in terror, believing Thame had summoned an ocular curse.
Kuran bolted toward the bamboo aqueduct, splashing icy water over his face and blowing through his nose, groaning loudly across the camp.
Little Tara, peeking out from behind a timber post, burst into giggles before an elder woman swiftly pulled her back inside.
Elya, standing upwind on higher ground, stared at the scene with wide, rapt fascination.
She witnessed how a single splash of this "red water" utterly incapacitated a veteran chieftain within seconds, without shedding a drop of blood.
"Orok... Spray them... Blind eyes... All stop!" Elya pointed at the pressure tank, her eyes flashing with keen predatory insight.
"Exactly, Elya..." Thame wiped his streaming tears with a wry grin. "When they pack into the gorge, one blast of this and their charge is finished!"
The next critical objective was outfitting their frontline defenders with close-combat body armor.
Thame walked over to the shredded rear dual wheels of the ten-wheeler, destroyed during the plunge off the mountain pass.
Modern heavy commercial truck radial tires are constructed from fifteen-millimeter vulcanized rubber reinforced with interwoven high-tensile steel belts.
Engineered to withstand ten tons of axle load and over one hundred PSI of internal pressure, they possessed unmatched puncture resistance.
Thame wielded the SK5 folding saw alongside his heavy carbon Bowie knife, sawing out curved rectangular tread plates shaped to fit a human chest.
Using the 20V cordless hammer drill, he bored clean perimeter holes, lacing them with 100-pound PE line and 550 paracord.
Within two hours, four sets of "Steel-Belted Radial Chest Cuirasses" and matching forearm vambraces were successfully fabricated.
Kuran donned the jet-black, ridged rubber cuirass, drew his own flint-tipped spear, and thrust it directly into his chest with full force.
*Thud! Crack!*
The brittle flint tip shattered into shards upon impacting the dense rubber tread and internal steel-wire mesh.
The tire tread bore scarcely a scratch; Kuran felt no shock transmitted through the dense vulcanized barrier.
The Cliff Deer hunters erupted into thunderous cheers, hailing the tire armor as the "Invincible Dragon Skin."
Thame handed the second pressurized sprayer to Kuran, ordering two buckets of fresh water kept behind the gate for emergency eye-washing.
Moka began escorting little Tara, the women, and the elders up the cliff trail toward the Secret Cliff Sanctuary with water gourds and smoked meat.
Elya strapped on a radial forearm guard, adjusted her composite recurve bow, and checked the quiver of twenty flint arrows slung over her back.
She met Thame's gaze with unwavering, unspoken trust before climbing to the high sentry ledge overlooking the chokepoint.
Thame checked the Smith & Wesson .38 revolver nestled in his chest holster; all 43 rounds remained safely accounted for.
He reaffirmed his ironclad resolve never to pull the trigger unless faced with an absolute, fatal catastrophe.
Thame climbed to the cab roof, refreshing the online war room thread on his flickering 2G phone.
`@CombatMedic_US`: *`Brilliant move with the Mala chili! Capsaicin aerosol in a narrow chokepoint will rout the frontline 100%, but beware wind shifts and keep rinse water ready!`*
`@VikingBushcraft`: *`Radial tire armor makes stone spears obsolete; paleolithic flint cannot penetrate modern steel-ply radial belts!`*
The eight warriors, outfitted with rubber cuirasses and gripping their spears and axes, took position behind the zigzag barricade in soaring morale.
A tense, absolute silence descended over Fortress Alpha, until the frantic thumping of every defender's heart was audible in the chill air.
*Tweet! Tweet! Tweet!*
Suddenly, three piercing blasts from the titanium whistle echoed down from Elya's sentry perch high atop the limestone cliff!
The triple blast signaled the immediate arrival of the enemy warband!
Thame raised his monocular toward the southern limestone ridges three kilometers away, cutting through the midday sun glare.
Along the crest, savage pelt banners fluttered amidst billowing dust clouds,
And the deep, hollow roar of mammoth-bone war horns reverberated across the canyon—the thirty-man vanguard of the Demon Fang Clan had arrived!
