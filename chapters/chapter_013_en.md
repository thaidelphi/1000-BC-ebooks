# Chapter 13: The Chokepoint Ambush and the Barbed Wire Trap

The midnight silence of the horseshoe limestone amphitheater was wrapped in a biting 5°C wind.
A fragile glaze of hoarfrost began to crystalize over the ten-wheeler's windshield and dry grass around the camp.
The wind whistled through the forty-meter limestone fissures, sounding like the warning whispers of ancestral spirits.
Astride the steel container roof, Thame and Elya stood side by side, braving the glacial draft on watch.
The huntress scanned the darkness below with the razor-sharp senses of an apex predator on full alert.
Suddenly, atop the three-meter timber palisade overlooking the narrow chokepoint,
The passive infrared motion sensor (PIR) of the solar floodlight detected incoming body heat.
*Click!*
*Flash!*
Blinding daylight-white illumination at 6,500 Kelvin erupted from the 200-watt SMD 5730 LED array.
A piercing beam cut through the night fog, illuminating the terrain for more than fifty meters ahead.
In a split second, the ground before the barricade was bathed in brilliance as bright as high noon.
And right in the center of that beam, three hunched figures clad in shaggy carnivore pelts recoiled in utter terror!
They were scout-hunters from the cannibalistic Demon Fang Clan, their faces smeared with soot and ritual scars.
Their dilated pupils, tuned to the pitch-black wilderness, were instantly struck by an overwhelming deluge of photons.
Acute flashblindness overwhelmed their optic nerves without warning.
Their irises seized violently; their visual field fractured into blinding white glare and disorienting violet afterimages.
"Ga-rok! Ko-tar!"
Frantic shrieks echoed off the cliff face as the terrified scouts believed the ancient beast had opened its solar eye of divine judgment.
The lead scout, wearing the skull of a sabertooth cat strapped across his shoulder, lost his wits in the panic.
He swung his chipped quartzite hand-axe wildly and charged blindly forward to escape the blazing light.
Yet that desperate charge carried him straight into the zigzag low-wire entanglement trap Thame and Kuran had concealed beneath the withered shrubs.
*Snag! Rip!*
Four-point galvanized steel barbs bit hard into his rawhide ankle wraps and tore deep into his shin flesh.
The violent momentum pulled the modern barbed wire taut, twisting its razor-sharp steel teeth deep into his calf muscles.
The harder he thrashed, the deeper the unforgiving steel hooks sank into his skin.
"Aaaargh!" The muscular scout collapsed face-first onto the frozen mud, his dark blood staining the frost and moraine rubble.
The second scout, clutching a bone-tipped spear, lunged forward to drag his thrashing comrade free.
Yet his bare hands gripped directly onto the coiled barbed wire with full force.
The modern zinc-coated steel sliced across his palms and forearms, scattering droplets of crimson across the frost.
He gasped in agony, dropped his spear, and stumbled backward in sheer, paralyzed horror.
In their primitive minds, this was no weapon crafted by man—it was a living, flesh-devouring demon vine that fed on human blood.
High on the container roof, the 1,500-lumen white beam of Elya's headlamp pinned the scouts firmly in place.
The huntress dropped to one knee upon the steel roof, drawing her recurve bow back to full draw weight.
A jet-black flint arrowhead remained locked squarely onto the exposed throat of the blinded scout below.
"Orok... (Enemy...)" Elya hissed through clenched teeth, her eyes blazing with lethal hunter's resolve.
Her three fingers were tensed, ready to release the deadly shaft at the slightest twitch.
Down below, Kuran and two veteran Cliff Deer warriors burst from behind the zigzag stone wall.
They raised heavy ash-wood spears tipped with flint blades, poised to impale the intruders where they lay.
"Tah! Tah! (Stop!)" Thame's thunderous shout split the freezing night through the open cab window.
"Sua! Sua! (Friend... Don't shoot!) Kuran! Elya! Tah! (Hold your weapons!)"
Thame knew that dead corpses were worthless compared to live captives.
Gaining intelligence on the enemy's numbers, vanguard route, and attack timeline was a matter of life and death for the fortress.
Elya froze her fingers upon the bowstring, her dark brows furrowing in frustration.
Yet seeing the resolute, commanding authority in Thame's eyes, she slowly eased her draw.
Kuran thrust up his weathered hand, signaling his spearmen to halt their tips barely inches from the enemy's chests.
The third scout, who had escaped injury, threw down his serrated bone dagger and prostrated himself upon the frozen ground, trembling before the light.
Thame leaped down the truck's cab steps, his goose down coat rustling in the wind.
His right hand rested firmly upon the grip of the Smith & Wesson .38 revolver concealed beneath his jacket, alert for any ambush.
"Standard vines or bark cordage will never hold them; these brutes have monstrous strength and can gnaw through natural fiber," Thame calculated rapidly.
He hurried toward the rear cargo container and extracted two compact parcels pre-sorted beside the aisle.
The first bore tracking code `#TH-229104` (0.6 kg), shipped from a Doi Tao wilderness fishing depot.
A flick of his blade slit the packing tape to reveal an industrial spool of **8-strand braided PE fishing line (100 lb test, 300 meters)**.
Constructed of ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene, this dark green braid was engineered to withstand monstrous river predators.
Human teeth could never chew through it, and its tensile strength exceeded fifty kilograms per strand.
The second parcel bore tracking code `#TH-391024` (1.8 kg) from a hardware and survival supplier.
Inside lay a **dual-grit diamond sharpening bench stone (400/1000 mesh)** along with a **pouch of activated biochar**.
Thame sliced off four-meter lengths of the PE braid, handing them to Kuran and the warriors to bind the scouts' wrists and ankles behind their backs.
The tough polyethylene cord was whipped around their limbs in multiple tight hitches; any struggle only tightened the bite without snapping the line.
Donning heavy leather work gloves, Thame and Kuran carefully pried the lead scout's calf from the barbed wire teeth.
The laceration was long and painful, but had missed the major femoral artery; Thame applied biochar powder and sterile gauze to staunch the bleeding.
Thame swept his flashlight beam over the confiscated armaments: chipped quartzite hand-axes, bone daggers, and rank dried meat pouches.
Yet as the white beam swept across the lead scout's chest, Thame's breath hitched violently.
Hanging from the scout's sinew necklace, alongside predatory fangs and dried human finger joints...
Hung a **spent brass cartridge casing of 9mm Luger**, drilled through the case wall and strung as a talisman!
The headstamp stamped into the brass rim was unmistakable: *WIN 9mm LUGER*—an authentic artifact from the twenty-first century!
Even worse, the short thrusting spear sheathed at his hip was not tipped with chipped stone,
It bore a **crudely hammered copper blade lashed to the wooden shaft with a bright yellow industrial nylon zip-tie**!
An icy chill surged up Thame's spine, far colder than the glacial breeze of the Younger Dryas.
This was not merely a primitive skirmish—another human from the twenty-first century was actively weaponizing and arming this cannibal horde!
Thame shoved the brass casing directly before the captive's face, demanding with sharp, fierce gestures: "Who gave you this?! Who sent you?!"
The trussed scout stared at the brass glinting in Thame's hand, his eyes instantly igniting with fanatical, cultish fervor.
He spat bloody phlegm onto the frost-rimed rock, his chest heaving with manic laughter.
Then, in a terrifying collision of guttural clicks and clumsy English consonants, the savage shrieked into the night:
"Vic... tor! Thunder God Victor! Kraak... Victor... Marn! Coming to butcher and devour you all!"
"Victor...?" Thame repeated the name, frozen in sheer disbelief.
A modern Western name, enunciated from the jagged teeth of an Ice Age cannibal ten thousand years in the past!
The scout raved that Warlord Kraak and the Thunder God Victor were marching with a hundred spears to wipe Fortress Alpha off the earth.
Thame scrambled atop the truck's cab, raising his smartphone to catch the flickering one-bar 2G signal.
He snapped a photo of the 9mm brass casing and the yellow nylon zip-tie, posting the image alongside the name "Victor" to the emergency forum thread.
Responses from `@CombatMedic_US` and `@AstroNerd_Munich` pinged back within seconds:
*`[CODE RED ALERT] Authentic 9mm brass! And breaking news from the storm reports: an 'Armored Special Prison Transport' carrying an ex-mercenary convict named Victor vanished on the exact same mountain highway!`*
*`[TACTICAL WARNING] Cliff Deer only has ten warriors. If a hundred cannibals charge the gate, you'll be buried under a human wave! You need asymmetric crowd control and impenetrable body armor immediately!`*
Thame lowered the phone, gazing out past the illumination into the pitch-black southern ridges.
The preparation clock had run out; one hundred enemy spears were marching through the gorge,
And tomorrow's dawn would mark the beginning of an existential war for human survival!
