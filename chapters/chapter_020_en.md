# Chapter 20: The Desperate Race, Dr. Nicha's Clinic & First Peaceful Contact

The fifteen-kilometer return trek across the rugged Western Pass was devoid of any paths or trails, littered with jagged granite scree and ice crevasses that made any sledge or cart utterly useless.

Dr. Nicha had to tightly immobilize her sprained right ankle with elastic compression wraps and heavy-duty duct tape, locking the joint so she could limp under her own power without rolling it.

Thame handed over his adjustable aluminum trekking pole to serve as her crutch alongside a sturdy pine staff carved by Elya, while Thame shouldered the 65-liter pack lashed with the heavy medical chest.

Hobbling step by agonizing step across the roadless alpine terrain, their pace slowed to a crawl, forcing a mandatory bivouac night beneath their previous cliff overhang to boil venison and recuperate.

At dawn on Day 15, they pushed forward from first light, Thame and Elya taking turns supporting Dr. Nicha over the rocky shelves along their previous tracks.

After nearly nine hours of grueling trek, they finally crested the final limestone col in the late afternoon, revealing the natural horseshoe basin of Fortress Alpha below.

The formidable timber palisade and gentle curls of warm chimney smoke caught the pale afternoon sun, standing as a miraculous sanctuary amidst the primeval wilderness.

"Is that... is that truly your iron fortress?!" Dr. Nicha gasped, her breath billowing in white clouds as her eyes widened in profound disbelief.

Before her lay not the crude brushwood lean-tos or damp caves of primitive troglodytes, but a formidable timber bastion ringed by tiered barbed wire defenses.

The rhythmic rush of cascading water drove the modified Pelton hydro-turbine, spinning double-reduction pulleys with a muted mechanical hum to supply round-the-clock 24V DC power to the camp's solar lanterns.

An elevated, eighty-meter split-bamboo aqueduct—the "Green Dragon Aqueduct"—channeled pristine alpine springwater from the sheer north cliff directly into a large cistern beside the 25-ton Hino 500 Victor truck fortress.

And curling lazily from the clay chimney of the twin Rocket Stoves inside the longhouses were plumes of clean, virtually smokeless heat, promising refuge from the subzero chill.

"Sua! Sua! (Friends! Allies!)" Khuran's thunderous war cry echoed from the narrow four-meter chokepoint entrance.

The towering hunter, clad in his fifteen-millimeter steel-belted radial tire chest armor, bounded forward in welcoming joy alongside young Tara, who jumped and waved his arms wildly.

"Brother Thame! Sister Elya!" Tara shouted, throwing his arms tightly around Thame's waist, tears of sheer relief streaking through the grime on the boy's cheeks.

Shaman Moka and the thirty souls of the Cliff Deer Clan emerged from the winter longhouses, gathering around the returning trio with joyous cheers and outstretched hands.

Thame introduced Dr. Nicha using calculated sign gestures and established vocabulary: "Nicha... Shaman... Sua (Friend)... here to heal our wounded."

To strip the biting frost that numbed their extremities, Thame provided a clean towel and dry spare clothes from the truck cab before guiding Dr. Nicha up the cliff ledges into the **Heaven's Cliff Secret Cave** through the vine curtain.

Stepping inside, an ambient warmth of 24°C and the soothing mineral aroma of the **40°C emerald hot spring pool** enveloped them like a divine embrace.

Left in total privacy, Dr. Nicha soaked blissfully in the warm mineral spring, washing away half a month of soot and ice before changing into fresh clothes and donning her clean white clinical coat.

"Simply incredible, Thame... how on earth did you create an oasis like this in the Ice Age?" Dr. Nicha praised with a rejuvenated, bright smile.

Once their stamina was rekindled, Thame and Khuran pulled on heavy leather gloves and descended to inspect the readiness of the **three-tier barbed wire perimeter** guarding the chokepoint.

In a paleolithic era where predators and raiders knew only flint and bone, high-tensile galvanized barbed wire was Fortress Alpha's undisputed primary defense asset.

Thame inspected Tier 1: low-tangle tripwires strung in a zigzag pattern fifteen to thirty centimeters above ground, concealed beneath dead pine needles to trip charging beasts and attackers.

Should dire wolves or cave bears charge blindly, the four-point steel barbs would tear through thick pelts to puncture noses, lips, and paw pads, triggering uncontrollable panic and retreat.

Tier 2 formed the main five-to-six strand barricade choking the narrow defile, funneling any breach attempt into a lethal kill zone that stone axes could never sever.

And Tier 3 comprised dense coils of concertina wire crowning the three-meter timber stockade, forbidding sabertooth cats from vaulting the perimeter.

"The wire is taut and combat-ready at every post, Khuran," Thame affirmed, clapping the warrior's armored shoulder as Khuran nodded with steely resolve.

Next, Thame and Dr. Nicha moved to establish a **"Sterile Field Clinic"** within the forward bulkhead of the Hino 500 container.

Clear poly-sheeting was suspended as an isolation curtain, the floor sealed with washable waterproof tarps and illuminated by glare-free, smokeless warm-white solar camping lanterns.

Their first patients were Khuran, nursing a deep laceration on his forearm, and two severely wounded refugees from the prior skirmish whose festering flint shrapnel wounds were swelling with infection.

In a primitive tribe, such trauma meant agonizing restraints while crude stone blades gouged flesh, often leading to fatal septic shock.

Instead, Dr. Nicha uncapped a sterile syringe and expertly infiltrated the margins with **2% Lidocaine local anesthetic**.

Within three minutes, excruciating agony evaporated into complete numbness; Khuran stared in jaw-dropping astonishment as the scalpel incised his flesh without a whisper of pain.

Using sterile tissue forceps, Dr. Nicha teased two jagged flint splinters from the deep muscle bed, irrigated the cavity with sterile saline, applied antibiotic ointment, and closed with neat nylon sutures.

Shaman Moka, witnessing the procedure, dropped his sacred rattle and dropped to his knees before Dr. Nicha in overwhelming reverence.

"Heavenly medicine that slays pain... cutting flesh without a single tear!" Moka chanted, proclaiming Dr. Nicha the **"White-Gowned Shaman"** gifted with the power of life.

Thame and Khuran immediately declared Dr. Nicha the official **Chief Medical Officer** of Fortress Alpha.

Stepping out of the container clinic, Thame caught sight of two sentries shivering violently atop the timber stockade amidst the dropping -8°C mountain wind.

Maintaining twelve warriors across three four-man shifts in subzero blizzards was sapping tribal strength and courting hypothermia.

Relying on his mechanical engineering background, Thame resolved to revolutionize camp security through electro-mechanical adaptation.

Climbing the stockade post by the chokepoint, he removed the water-sealed casing of the 200W solar floodlight equipped with an **infrared PIR motion sensor**.

The sensor possessed a wide 120-degree detection cone with an active thermal range of eight to ten meters.

Using his rescue multitool, Thame spliced jumper wires from the PIR trigger output terminal through an automotive relay switch module.

He then routed an insulated wire across the camp palisade, leading directly beneath the cab of the Hino 500 truck.

The wire terminated at the **24V dual air horn solenoid valve**, plumbed directly to the truck's 6.6-bar compressed air reservoir.

This improvised circuit birthed a devastating **"Dual-Sensory Automated Perimeter Alarm"**:

Whenever a warm-blooded entity breached the eight-meter threshold, the PIR sensor instantly triggered a blinding 200W Daylight 6,500K flash of pure illumination.

Simultaneously, the relay tripped the solenoid, unleashing a concussive **130 dB acoustic shockwave** from the dual air horns in sharp one-to-two-second pulse blasts.

With this autonomous trap operational, Fortress Alpha reduced freezing sentry duty down to a single watchman stationed on the truck cab roof.

The remaining warriors transitioned to a **Standby Quick Reaction Force (QRF)**, resting warmly in the longhouses beside the Rocket Stoves.

Should danger approach, the 130 dB acoustic blast would roust the entire camp into combat positions in under five seconds.

Dusk descended rapidly, cloaking the horseshoe gorge in pitch darkness as the mercury plummeted to -9°C.

Scout reports confirmed that the main Beast-Fang cannibal host remained immobilized by severe blizzards in the southern passes, granting Fortress Alpha crucial breathing room.

Then, out of the silent glacial night...

*FLASHHHHH!*

The 200W floodlight above the chokepoint erupted like a miniature sun, obliterating the pitch-black darkness!

*HONNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNK—BLARRRRRRRR!*

The 130 dB dual truck air horns bellowed with deafening, earth-shaking thunder, their sound waves ricocheting violently off the forty-meter limestone cliffs!

The autonomous warning system had executed its maiden trigger in paleolithic history!

Within five seconds, the longhouse doors burst open as Khuran and his radial-armored warriors surged out with crossbows and barbed pikes at the ready.

Thame seized his 12x50 monocular, scaled the cab roof, and peered through the blazing glare toward the chokepoint entrance.

Yet what materialized within the piercing beam was neither a dire wolf pack nor bloodthirsty raiders...

But the lone figure of an elderly primitive man prostrating face-down in the snow, hands clutching his head in absolute terror.

"Spare me... O Great Sun God..." the elder wept, trembling uncontrollably, his ragged furs revealing a starving frame of exposed ribs.

Elya locked her gaze onto the curved ram-horn totems carved upon his chest, tugging Thame's arm: "Elder Bathor!... Chieftain of the Mountain Sheep Clan!"

Bathor frantically pointed back into the frozen darkness, stammering that his eleven surviving clan members—women, elders, and infants—lay hiding in a rock cleft, freezing to death.

Terrified of leading his family into an enemy den, he had risked his life to scout the miraculous light alone, desperate to plead for warmth and scraps of food to save the dying children.

"Khuran, take your warriors and bring the children and elders inside immediately!" Thame lowered his weapon and commanded firmly.

Within moments, the warriors carried the trembling children and supported the frostbitten women into the sanctuary, saving all eleven souls from the icy night.

Thame and Dr. Nicha hurried to the rear of the container to unearth emergency relief supplies.

Thame's flashlight beam scanned the parcel stacks, locking onto a sturdy corrugated carton bearing waybill `#TH-990128`.

The manifest label was unmistakable: **"San Pa Tong Community Health Center — Primary Healthcare & High-Altitude Rescue Support Kit."**

Thame drew his utility knife and sliced cleanly through the reinforced packing tape.

Inside lay **ten silver reflective Mylar emergency space blankets**, sterile trauma dressings, sterile cotton, elastic compression bandages, and pediatric antipyretics.

"Exactly what we need!" Dr. Nicha exclaimed in triumphant relief.

Thame, Dr. Nicha, and young Tara moved among the shivering refugees, wrapping each child and elder in radiant thermal foil blankets.

The Mylar film trapped and reflected ninety percent of their radiated body heat, arresting severe hypothermia within minutes.

Tara brought forward steaming bamboo cups of rich venison broth ladled from the titanium pot, the fragrant warmth melting the last vestiges of terror.

Tears of profound gratitude coursed down the weathered cheeks of the refugees; Fortress Alpha had proven itself the greatest humanitarian sanctuary of the ancient world.

Yet in the dead of night, as both clans rested peacefully around the glowing hearths...

Thame and Dr. Nicha, keeping watch on the container roof, observed a bizarre anomaly twisting across the heavens above the black monolith ridge.

The upper atmosphere began rippling in fluid, watery distortions, causing the icy starlight to shimmer and fracture.

Along the northern horizon, ethereal ribbons of violet-cyan luminescence and prismatic magnetic halos began spiraling into a brilliant, glowing vortex.

The pungent odor of ozone drifted down upon the freezing wind, and Thame's magnetic compass needle spun erratically without heading.

A temporal rift was breathing open across the paleolithic sky!
