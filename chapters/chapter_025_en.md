# Chapter 25: The Great Siege & The Decisive Flare

The piercing blast of an animal horn echoed sharply across the limestone gorge in the early dawn, mingling with the bloodthirsty war cries of over a hundred warriors trampling through the virgin snow!

The grey winter mist hovering over the ancient taiga pines shattered, unveiling the savage host of the Beast Fang tribe pouring out of the southern mountain pass. Their bodies were smeared with black ash and crusted blood, clad in predator pelts and dire wolf skulls, brandishing bone-tipped lances, flint axes, and thick pine shields.

At the very front strode the towering, two-meter frame of War Chief Grak, his massive feet crunching into the frozen snow. Perched upon his broad shoulder was the fossilized skull of a giant sabertooth cat. In his right hand, he swung a long-hafted limestone war hammer, while his left gripped a barbed bone lance, his bloodshot eyes burning like those of a rabid beast.

And beside the savage warlord, lurking in the pine shadows... emerged a man clad in modern tactical trousers, a snow-camo parka, and muddy combat boots—Victor!

In the former mercenary’s hands rested a matte-black Remington 870 pump-action shotgun, with a Glock 17 holstered at his hip. Victor’s cold eyes locked onto Fortress Alpha with a cruel, predatory smirk.

Atop the Hino 500 ten-wheeler, behind the elevated sandbag bunker, Tham pressed the 12x50 monocular to his eye. Beside him knelt Elya, her recurve bow drawn taut with a razor-tipped carbon arrow. Below, behind the forty-centimeter-thick double-stockade wall packed with compacted earth and river gravel, Kuran and twenty Hill Deer hunters clad in steel-belted radial tire armor gripped their leaf-spring spears, their knuckles white with tension.

All the women, children, and elderly had been safely evacuated into the Secret Cliff Cave and the field medical bay behind the shipping container. The entire outpost stood dead silent, braced for an epoch-defining collision.

"Hahaha! Look at that pathetic wooden pen!" Victor’s harsh voice boomed across the subzero air through a handheld megaphone. "Hand over the ten-wheeler and all the cargo, Tham! Otherwise, I'll let these savages scalp every single one of you alive!"

Tham did not utter a single word. He merely peered through the 12mm-thick 5160 leaf-spring firing slit, tracking every shift in the enemy’s ranks with icy composure.

Met with absolute silence, Victor scoffed in contempt. He gave Grak a nod, raised the Remington 870 to his shoulder, and aimed squarely at the outer wooden palisade—eager to demonstrate his 'divine thunder' to send the primitive defenders fleeing in terror.

*Clack-clack!* Victor racked a nine-pellet buckshot round into the chamber and pulled the trigger!

*BOOOOOM!*

A blinding orange muzzle flash erupted from the 12-gauge barrel. The thunderous blast ripped through the limestone gorge like a point-blank lightning strike, spewing acrid white gunsmoke. A high-velocity swarm of lead pellets tore through the frozen air and slammed savagely into the front wall!

*Thud! Thump! Wham!*

Yet... the sound that followed was not the splintering crash of wood Victor had anticipated!

The supersonic lead pellets punched through the outer pine bark, but the moment they struck the forty-centimeter-thick core of compacted red clay and limestone gravel, over two thousand joules of kinetic energy were utterly swallowed. Millions of earth and rock granules crushed the soft lead flat, halting the pellets dead within ten centimeters of impact. Only a harmless puff of red dust drifted into the freezing wind. The bulwark did not yield a single millimeter!

The smirk on Victor’s face froze solid. The mercenary’s eyes stretched wide in disbelieving horror:
"Sandbags?!... Bullshit, where the hell did you bastards get sandbags in the middle of the Stone Age?! And who the fuck thought of packing dirt to stop bullets?!"

Behind the ramparts, the Hill Deer hunters, who had instinctively flinched at the terrifying thunderclap, looked up to see their wall completely unbreached. Kuran ran his calloused hand across the rough sandbags, throwing his head back to roar at the heavens with unshakeable faith:

"Earth... Stone... Devours Death! Sua! Wa!"

The primal terror of the fire-spitting thunder wand evaporated in an instant, replaced by blazing confidence in the hearts of every warrior!

Tham raised the 50W megaphone, triggering a piercing wail of the electronic emergency siren that echoed thunderously across the valley to panic the savages, before booming directly at Victor:
"Victor! Your shotgun can't pierce packed earth! Order your men to fall back, or don't say I didn't warn you!"

Seeing that the white-faced man's magic weapon had failed to splinter the gate, and stung by the booming voice from the fortress, War Chief Grak flew into an uncontrollable berserk fury. He hoisted his colossal stone hammer overhead and bellowed a guttural roar:

"Orok! Tor! Marn!"

Over a hundred Beast Fang warriors shrieked in savage unison, surging forward like a howling human wave directly into the four-meter bottleneck chokepoint before the gate!

This was precisely the killing ground Tham had engineered!

As the frenzied horde charged blindly into the funnel... the first fifteen warriors caught their shins on the low fifteen-centimeter tripwire, tumbling headfirst into the snow. Their momentum propelled them straight into the double concertina coils of high-tensile galvanized barbed wire arranged in an interlocking zigzag!

*Riiip! Slash! Aaaaagh!*

Razor-sharp four-point steel barbs ripped viciously into bare skin, thighs, calves, and torsos. The more the savages thrashed, the deeper the steel teeth bit and clamped. The charging ranks behind them, unable to halt on the slick ice, crashed into the pile-up, entangling themselves in the razor web. The mighty horde collapsed into a screaming, writhing heap of human flesh, shrieking in agony!

Atop the truck roof, Elya held her precious carbon arrows strictly in reserve, leading the rampart archers to loose a barrage of flint-tipped hunting arrows through the leaf-spring slits, methodically suppressing warriors attempting to scale the rocky flanks.

Lurking in the rear, Victor cursed violently, drawing his Glock 17 to fire two rapid shots at the firing slits:
*Bang! Bang!*

The 9mm parabellum rounds struck the 12mm 5160 leaf-spring steel plates flanking the slit—*Spang! Spang!*—throwing sparks, but the automotive spring steel deflected the copper bullets without a scratch!

"Damn it! It won't penetrate!" Victor swore, conscious of his dwindling ammunition reserves.

Yet on the battlefield, War Chief Grak was lost to berserk bloodlust. Oblivious to his men’s agony, the giant trampled over the tangled bodies of his own warriors, vaulted the flanking boulder, and charged the palisade gate alone!

"GRAAAAK!" the monster roared, swinging his twenty-pound limestone war hammer with bone-crushing ferocity against the main timber gate post!

*CRRRRRAAAASH!*

The colossal blow shattered an eight-inch pine timber. The crossbeam groaned under immense stress. Two more strikes, and the timber gate would collapse, allowing eighty warriors to flood Fortress Alpha in a bloody close-quarters massacre!

Kuran and the Cliff Clan warriors thrust their spears through the timber gaps, but Grak swatted the stone points aside with his thick, pelt-wrapped forearms, cocking the limestone hammer back for another crushing blow!

The fatal crisis exploded before their eyes—one more strike would breach the palisade gate wide open!

Tham did not hesitate for a microsecond. Casting the megaphone aside onto the truck roof, he reached down and seized the decisive trump card he had unboxed from the heavy orange waterproof case `#TH-001928` and staged right beside the sandbag parapet during evening preparations.

It was a heavy-duty, break-action 26.5mm Marine Distress Flare Gun molded from high-impact international orange polymer—already pre-loaded with a massive High-Intensity Red Aerial Distress Cartridge ready for immediate discharge! Packed with magnesium and strontium nitrate, the cartridge was engineered to burn at an incandescent 3,000 degrees Fahrenheit (1,650°C) with a blinding output exceeding 30,000 candela.

With his right thumb, Tham pulled the external hammer back until it locked with a crisp *clack!* Resting the heavy orange barrel firmly across the sandbags, he brought the wide bore into dead center alignment with War Chief Grak’s chest from barely twelve meters away!

"Grak! Look up here!" Tham’s voice cut through the clamor like an executioner's bell.

The savage warlord paused, his blood-splattered visage snapping upward to meet Tham’s icy gaze. Grak's eyes reflected the strange, heavy-gauge orange tube.

Tham did not hesitate for a microsecond. He squeezed the trigger!

*Thump-whsssssh-BOOOOOOM!*

The primer detonated with a crisp recoil, unleashing a screaming sphere of incandescent red magnesium fire the size of a man’s fist! Blazing like a miniature fallen sun, the crimson fireball hurtled through the freezing air and slammed directly into Grak’s chest with pinpoint perfection!

*KABOOOOM!*

The 3,000°F chemical furnace erupted upon contact with the sabertooth skull and cured wolf pelts!

The fossilized bone shattered into showers of white-hot sparks. Grak’s heavy fur mantle burst into an inferno of roaring crimson flames in an instant. The agonizing heat and chest impact staggered the two-meter-tall, hundred-kilogram warlord, knocking him flat on his back into the deep snow!

Over thirty thousand candela of blinding red radiance washed over the frozen valley, turning the white snowfields into an incandescent sea of blood-red brilliance. Flocks of wild birds scattered in terror as the entire battlefield was enveloped in divine, blinding solar fire!

"AAAAAAARGH! Tor! Tor!... Aka! Aka!"

Grak shrieked in absolute torment and horror, thrashing frantically in the deep snowbank to smother the flames consuming his furs. His war hammer and bone lance flew from his grip, his ferocious aura shattered into pitiful whimpering in the blink of an eye!

In that frozen second... the remaining eighty Beast Fang warriors stood paralyzed, their eyes wide with marrow-deep terror.

Their invincible chieftain, whom they regarded as a demigod of slaughter, had just been struck down by a man who pointed an orange rod and commanded the living fire of the sun to burn him alive. This was no longer a battle between men—this was the wrath of the Sun God!

*Clatter! Clatter! Thud!*

Flint spears, bone axes, and stone daggers dropped simultaneously into the snow!

Every single warrior—both those tangled in the razor coils and those standing in the rear—fell to their knees in the freezing slush. They pressed their foreheads against the earth, weeping and shivering, groveling in unconditional surrender before Fortress Alpha!

Watching from afar, Victor’s face drained of every drop of color. A chill of sheer dread seized his spine as he stared at the smoking flare gun and Tham’s unflinching gaze atop the impenetrable bunker. The ex-mercenary realized in cold certainty that he could never defeat this man or this fortress. Turning on his heel, Victor fled in terror, vanishing into the southern pine wilderness!

A deafening roar of triumph erupted from Kuran and the Hill Deer hunters along the parapets:
"Tor!... Tham!... Mahatheva!"

Dr. Nicha, carrying sterile trauma shears and Mebo burn ointment, sprinted from the gate to secure and treat the second-degree burns on Grak’s chest as he lay groaning in the snow. Meanwhile, Borg and his eight pine scouts emerged from the southern ridge, helping to round up and disarm the hundred surrendered captives.

The Battle of the Bulwark ended in decisive, absolute triumph for Fortress Alpha... with zero defender casualties, and every single one of the forty-three rounds of .38 Special preserved completely intact!

As gentle snowflakes began to drift down over the blackened snow and spent smoke, Tham lowered the flare gun, gazing past the prostrate army toward the lone set of combat bootprints disappearing into the southern crags...

The savage horde had been broken, but the shadow of a modern predator still stalked the prehistoric wilderness!
