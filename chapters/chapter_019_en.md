# Chapter 19: The Fallen Iron Beast & The Rescue of Dr. Nicha

Late morning sunlight glinted brilliantly off the snowpack draped over the limestone crags, the post-blizzard wind dying down to the steady crunch of Vibram soles against hard-packed ice.

Thame and Elya traversed the high western pass, ascending past eighteen hundred meters above sea level.

The thin, desiccated alpine air demanded labored breaths from their lungs, yet the crystal-clear atmosphere granted an unbroken panoramic view across the jagged limestone cordillera.

Following the fracture lines of a sunken glacial gorge, Thame caught sight of unmistakable anomalies along the frozen scree: shattered fragments of tempered automotive safety glass shimmering like rock salt, and deep gouge marks left by steel rims against the granite boulders.

Aggressive all-terrain tire treads pressed into the glazed ice shelf confirmed a heavy vehicle hurtling out of control before plunging into the abyss.

Black plastic bumper shards and trails of leaked hydraulic fluid led the eye down into the precipitous chasm.

"There... the iron beast that lies upon its back!" Elya pointed eagerly toward the shadowy crevasse below.

Wedged tight within a snow-choked glacial cleft, a dark blue vehicle lay overturned with its wheels pointing toward the sky, pinned between colossal granite slabs.

It was a lifted Toyota Hilux Revo 4WD pickup, outfitted with heavy steel roll bars and an expedition snorkel, its cab roof caved inward and windshield shattered.

Thame scrambled down the steep scree slope with calculated care, conducting an immediate, thorough inspection of the wreckage.

Both front tires were shredded and the rear leaf springs buckled, yet along the passenger door gleamed a distinct green agency seal: **"Department of National Parks, Wildlife and Plant Conservation — Mobile Wildlife Epidemiology Unit."**

The faint odor of vaporized gasoline and motor oil hung in the frozen air, mingled with the scent of long-extinguished scorched wire.

Dried blood crusted across the driver's seat, but there was no corpse; only indications of an expedition pack hastily dragged free, and the distinct waffle-soled tracks of hiking boots scrambling frantically toward an upper snow cave.

The stride was uneven and erratic, suggesting an injury to the leg or ankle.

"A survivor! A modern government official!" Thame's pulse quickened with profound hope.

*BANG!*

Abruptly, the sharp acoustic crack of a high-pressure pneumatic dart gun detonated through the frozen defile roughly two hundred meters uphill!

The natural amphitheater of the sheer cliff faces amplified the gunshot like sudden thunder beneath the blue sky.

Immediately following came the bloodthirsty snarling of a predator pack and the desperate, defiant shriek of a woman shouting in Thai: "Get back! You filthy beasts!"

Thame and Elya locked eyes for a fraction of a second, drew their weapons, and sprinted up the snow-covered talus slope.

Their boots pumped furiously across slick scree, vaulting frozen meltwater channels with agile, unyielding determination.

The burning burn in their leg muscles was swallowed by the sheer adrenaline of a life-or-death emergency.

Reaching the flat rock shelf outside a narrow fissure cave... the scene that greeted them made Thame's blood boil.

A young woman clad in a blood-streaked, soot-stained khaki field jacket stood cornered, her back pinned rigidly against the sheer granite wall.

Her face was smeared with sweat and grime, her sharp features behind scratch-resistant safety goggles tight with panic, yet her gaze blazed with stubborn resolve.

She was **Dr. Nicha (a wildlife veterinarian and field epidemiologist, age 29)**, clutching a Pneu-Dart pneumatic projector whose pressure chamber held its final dart!

Circling before Dr. Nicha were five ravenous, silver-gray dire wolves baring dagger-like fangs, their scarred, massive alpha crouching low to spring at her exposed throat.

Viscous saliva dripped steadily from glistening jowls onto the blood-spotted snow, their guttural snarls vibrating the frigid air.

And more terrifying still... perched on a limestone overhang above the cave mouth were two Beast-Fang scouts armed with flint javelins and bone hatchets, watching the impending slaughter with cruel, mocking grins!

They were patiently waiting for the wolf pack to tear the prey apart before descending to loot the exotic prize.

"Elya! Drop the savages on the cliff first!" Thame roared without breaking stride.

*TWANG! THWACK!*

A high-velocity carbon arrow with an armor-piercing steel broadhead lanced through the air, punching clean through the first scout's chest and sending him plummeting off the cliff to smash dead against the boulders below!

The second scout shrieked in terror, his eyes bulging as his comrade died in a heartbeat, before turning and fleeing over the ridge in panicked terror.

In that exact split second, the dire wolf alpha launched into a ferocious, mid-air pounce directly toward Dr. Nicha!

"Dr. Nicha! EYES CLOSED!" Thame's voice cracked like thunder.

Wrenching the tactical package code `#TH-771920` from his pack, Thame held it locked in both hands!

It was a **Military-Grade Tactical LED Strobe Flashlight (2,000 Lumens)** machined from aerospace aluminum.

*STROBE! STROBE! STROBE!*

Thame slammed the switch into maximum frequency strobe, blasting two thousand lumens of blinding white light directly into the pack's dilated nocturnal pupils from five paces!

The disorienting optical overload shattered the night hunters' visual cortex in micro-seconds; the wolves howled in agony, tumbling sideways and crashing into the boulders in utter hysteria.

Mid-leap, the alpha lost all spatial orientation and slammed heavily into the snowbank at Dr. Nicha's feet.

Dr. Nicha did not waste the miraculous opening; gritting her teeth, she pulled the pneumatic trigger point-blank!

*PFFT! THUMP!*

A heavy-gauge veterinary immobilization dart sank deep into the alpha's jugular; the hyper-concentrated neuro-sedative took effect instantly, dropping the six-hundred-pound predator limp into the snow within seconds.

Elya loosed a second carbon arrow into the flank of the nearest wolf; the remaining predators, blinded and terrified, tucked their tails and bolted down the ravine in full retreat.

A profound, echoing silence settled over the snowbound shelf, punctuated only by the ragged breathing of the three survivors.

Dr. Nicha collapsed to her knees in the snow, the dart projector slipping from her trembling fingers as tears of overwhelming relief welled behind her goggles at the sound of her native language.

"You... you're Thai? There really are other survivors besides me..." Dr. Nicha wept, tears cutting clean tracks through the soot on her cheeks.

Thame knelt, gently lifting her to her feet and passing her an insulated flask of warm water: "You're safe now, Doctor. I'm Thame... We have a fortified encampment and allies waiting just behind us."

Dr. Nicha drank deeply, warmth and clarity slowly returning to her shivering body.

"I thought I was going to die alone in this frozen nightmare..." she whispered softly, clutching their hands in gratitude.

The meeting of two modern souls from the twenty-first century in the world of ten thousand BC was an invaluable miracle.

Dr. Nicha led Thame inside the shallow cave to retrieve a reinforced veterinary field trunk she had salvaged from the truck, packed with injectable anesthetics, antibiotics, syringes, surgical suture kits, and antivenom serums.

Also stowed inside were four spare sedative canisters and sterile scalpel blades that remained completely sealed.

Thame used heavy-duty zip ties to lock the chest down and secure the medical assets firmly to his expedition pack.

Yet as relief bloomed, Elya, who had climbed atop the black monolith rock spire to stand watch, called down in a strained, urgent voice.

"Thame... come see this! Quickly!"

Thame and Dr. Nicha scrambled up the steep stone spire.

Upon the flat summit, Thame felt a sharp, pungent stench of ozone crackling in the air, and static electricity made every hair on his arms stand on end.

Swirling at the center of the plateau was a residual, vortex-like shimmer of violet-indigo light hovering in mid-air—the unmistakable footprint of a **Temporal Rift**!

Thame pulled out his Blackview smartphone to open the magnetometer compass; the magnetic induction readings spiked into the red zone.

Cross-referencing the solar angle with the celestial alignments depicted in the cave murals of Fortress Alpha, Thame gasped as the truth struck home:

"This dimensional rift isn't closed permanently... it will tear open again during the upcoming **Total Solar Eclipse** in just a few days!"

Dr. Nicha's eyes widened in sheer disbelief: "You mean... we actually have a chance to go home?!"

"Yes... the gateway opens right here, and its time is fast approaching," Thame nodded firmly.

This was the solitary bridge back to the twenty-first century!

Yet in that exact moment, Elya raised the 12x50 monocular telescope toward the southern basin below, her entire frame locking rigid as stone.

Thame took the optic, aligning it with Elya's gaze...

The scene magnified twelve times through the emerald lens crushed their burgeoning hope under the grim shadow of imminent war.

Sprawled across the vast valley bowl below lay the sprawling war camp of the **Beast-Fang Cannibal Clan**!

Over a hundred hulking barbarian warriors were daubing their faces in fresh blood, hoisting copper-tipped spears bound with bright yellow nylon cable ties.

At the ceremonial clearing, massive timber totems stood ringed with animal skulls, while roaring sacrificial bonfires billowed black smoke into the sky.

And most critically... a monstrous warlord clad in beast hides was barking orders, directing battle lines to assemble battering logs and tar torches.

They were preparing to march their hundred-spear host to obliterate Fortress Alpha within forty-eight hours!

Two devastating truths stood laid bare: the gateway home was about to open... but Fortress Alpha and the people who trusted Thame were about to be massacred!

"If we just run home... Kuran, Tara, Moka... everyone in the camp will be slaughtered," Thame rasped, his knuckles whitening as his nails bit into his palms.

Dr. Nicha met Thame's gaze, her eyes blazing with fierce conviction: "I'm a doctor... I will never abandon my patients or the people who saved my life!"

Elya placed a firm hand upon Thame's shoulder, their eyes locking in profound trust that transcended ten millennia.

"We march back to camp right now! Before the war begins!" Thame commanded with iron resolve.

Tightening their packs and securing the medical trunk, the three set off at a dead sprint across the frozen ridges, racing back toward Fortress Alpha for the battle of their lives!
