# Chapter 6: The Trauma Medic of Two Worlds

The fragile warmth and camaraderie around the spruce campfire on the afternoon of the fourth day shattered in an instant as El-ya collapsed unconscious into the snow.

"El-ya! Open your eyes, my child, El-ya!" Kuran gathered his daughter’s limp frame into his arms, the weathered chieftain’s rough voice cracking with raw despair from the bottom of his heart.

Moga rushed forward in frantic agitation, his gnarled, trembling hands scooping up freezing muck and black, rotted peat from the edge of the glacial pool, preparing to smear the foul mud directly into the gaping wound to ward off evil spirits according to ancient custom.

"Stop! Don't touch that wound!" Thame lunged forward, his gloved hand clamping down hard on the old shaman’s wrist with all the strength he possessed.

In the Upper Paleolithic, poultices of river silt and decomposing moss were ancient rituals of desperate hope. In clinical reality, it was a lethal sentence—a direct inoculation of deadly tetanus spores and flesh-eating anaerobic bacteria straight into an open bloodstream.

Thame pivoted toward Kuran, barking short, sharp gestures and commands: "Pick her up! Bring her inside the truck! Move!"

Kuran hesitated for a fraction of a heartbeat. But looking into Thame’s calm, steel-eyed resolve and unmistakable sincerity, the chieftain gritted his teeth, scooped his daughter into his arms, and followed the young courier up the steel ladder into the rear of the cargo container.

Inside the enclosed container, the biting sub-arctic wind vanished, replaced by still, dry, draft-free shelter. The corrugated steel ribs served as an impenetrable protective bunker.

Thame cleared a stack of cardboard boxes and laid out Uncle Det’s down sleeping bag across the dry plywood floorboards. Kuran laid El-ya down with trembling gentleness.

The young huntress was burning like green wood in an open furnace. Her lips were parched, cracked, and flecked with dried blood; her respirations were rapid, shallow, and ragged enough to chill the soul.

Her right upper thigh was swollen taut as an overinflated tire, the discolored skin mottled with bruised purple and necrotic black. The four deep predator fang punctures oozed a putrid slurry of foul yellow pus and coagulated, dark venous blood. The stench of necrotic rot hung heavy in the air.

Thame recognized the clinical signs immediately: acute cellulitis and an extensive deep-tissue abscess caused by virulent anaerobic bacteria harbored in predatory saliva.

Without immediate surgical incision and drainage, the anaerobic bacteria would generate lethal tissue gas, destroying the limb and inducing terminal septic shock and multi-organ failure within twenty-four hours.

"Medical supplies... think, where are the medical supplies?!" Thame swept the beam of his tactical LED flashlight across the front wall of cargo crates in frantic haste.

His eyes snagged on a sturdy cardboard parcel bearing tracking barcode `#TH-339102`.

The thermal printed waybill read clearly: *Tactical Emergency Trauma & Combat First Aid Kit.*

With shaking fingers, Thame flicked open his utility blade and sliced through the reinforced fiber tape.

A high-visibility orange hard-shell case swung open, packed with professional-grade clinical equipment ready for immediate action:
- **Amoxicillin 500mg** capsules (1 blister strip of 10)
- **Paracetamol 500mg** tablets (1 blister strip of 10)
- **Povidone-Iodine 10% solution (Betadine)**, 100ml bottle
- **Sterile Disposable Scalpels No. 11**, 3 individually foil-wrapped units
- **Sterile Gauze Pads (4x4 inches)**, 10 sterile envelopes
- Surgical paper tape and elastic conforming compression bandages

"Everything I need... except I'm an automotive engineer, and I've never operated on a living human being in my entire life!" Thame cursed under his breath, his pulse hammering against his temples.

Grabbing his smartphone, he vaulted out of the steel box and scrambled up the exterior ladder to the cab roof to catch the edge of the sky.

The freezing gale tore at his jacket. Miraculously, the single flickering bar of 2G signal hovered against the bruised sky.

Thame snapped high-resolution photos of the necrotic wound and the pharmaceutical blister packs, punching out a frantic bulletin onto the survivalist forum thread:

*`[URGENT!] Paleolithic native female with severe predator bite abscess. Thigh taut, purple, foul pus drainage, septic fever, comatose. I have Amoxicillin 500mg, Betadine, No. 11 scalpels, sterile gauze. Any medics online, give me surgical instructions NOW or she dies tonight!`*

He hit submit. The seconds dragged like agonizing centuries against the howling wind.

Less than forty seconds later, an amber alert notification flashed across the screen.

`@CombatMedic_US:`
`"Listen to me carefully, kid! Former US Army flight medic here. That is a massive deep-space abscess with advancing necrotizing cellulitis from predatory oral anaerobic bacteria!"`

`"Golden Rule #1: DO NOT SUTURE OR CLOSE THAT WOUND! Those are anaerobic flesh-eating bacteria. If you seal the incision, the pocket becomes an oxygen-free incubator and gas gangrene will devour her leg within twelve hours!"`

`"Emergency Procedure:"`
`"1. Disinfect the entire circumference with iodine. Wash your hands in pure alcohol."`
`"2. Take the No. 11 scalpel. Locate the point of maximum fluctuation—where the skin is softest and thinnest. Make a vertical 1cm stab incision (Incision & Drainage). Push deep enough to penetrate the fascia. Express EVERY DROP of foul pus and necrotic hematoma until only fresh red blood flows."`
`"3. Dilute Betadine with boiled warm water until it looks like weak black tea. Irrigate the entire cavity aggressively."`
`"4. Pack the wound cavity loosely with Betadine-soaked sterile gauze wick to maintain continuous drainage."`
`"5. Administer a Loading Dose of 1,000mg Amoxicillin (2x 500mg capsules) orally IMMEDIATELY with 500mg Paracetamol. Then 500mg Amoxicillin every 8 hours for 7 consecutive days!"`
`"Move now, son, before that infection seeds her heart valves!"`

"Thank you, doc!" Thame shouted against the wind. He scrambled back down the ladder and threw himself back into the dry container.

He set the phone upright on a cardboard crate, angling the tactical LED work-light directly onto El-ya’s wounded thigh.

Thame poured rubbing alcohol over his bare hands, scrubbing vigorously, before tearing open the foil seal of the No. 11 surgical blade. The precision carbon steel gleamed sterile under the white beam.

He locked eyes with Kuran. "Hold her shoulders. Pin her uninjured leg. No matter what she does, do not let her thrash."

Kuran understood without words. The chieftain threw his full weight across his daughter’s chest and knee, his massive calloused hands locking like iron vices.

Thame took a slow, deep breath to steady his fingers. He positioned the pointed tip of the blade over the dark, purple-domed apex of the abscess.

Then, with clean, decisive pressure, he drove the blade in vertically.

*Spuuurt!*

A foul, pressurized fountain of grey-yellow pus, necrotic slough, and curdled dark blood erupted from the muscle pocket.

El-ya’s entire body arched in a violent, agonizing spasm. Her teeth ground together so fiercely that blood trickled from her lower lip. Her fingernails tore deep into the ripstop nylon of the sleeping bag, but through sheer primeval will, she refused to scream aloud.

Her staggering endurance tore at Thame’s conscience, but he did not hesitate.

With sterile gauze pads, he massaged and milked the tissue toward the puncture until the foul discharge gave way to bright arterial red. He poured boiled stream water—heated over the campfire and kept warm in Uncle Det’s vacuum thermos—into a clean steel cup, diluted it with Betadine until it was light tea-colored, and flushed the cavernous tract clean.

He packed a ribbon of iodized gauze deep into the wound pocket, leaving a wick extending outward, and bound the limb firmly with sterile cotton rolls.

Finally, Thame popped two red-and-yellow **Amoxicillin 500mg** capsules (1,000mg) and a white **Paracetamol** tablet from their foil blisters.

Supporting the back of El-ya’s neck, he slipped the three pills past her dry lips, trickling warm boiled water from the thermos cap into her mouth.

El-ya swallowed the modern medicine with labored gulps. Her fever-glazed eyes fluttered open, locking onto Thame’s face from inches away with emotions beyond words.

Thame pulled the lofted goose-down sleeping bag up to her chin, gently dabbing the cold sweat from her brow with a clean gauze pad.

"You're going to make it," Thame whispered softly. "Rest now."

Moga, who had been peering nervously through the container's steel doors, stared at the brightly colored capsules and the amber antiseptic with trembling awe.

In the old shaman’s eyes, this strange visitor had summoned no spirit totems; instead, he wielded sacred rustless steel and celestial elixirs to snatch El-ya’s soul back from the clutches of the underworld.

Two hours passed in quiet vigilance as the twilight deepened into blazing crimson and burnt orange across the horizon.

The miracles of twentieth-century pharmacology took root inside a body completely unmarred by prior antibiotic exposure.

Her scorching skin cooled as the antipyretic took effect. The shallow panting slowed into deep, rhythmic chest rises. A faint flush of healthy rose returned to her pale cheeks. The huntress slipped into the first restorative, healing sleep she had known in days.

Kuran, who had not looked away for a single second, dropped heavily to both knees before Thame.

Tears streamed unchecked down the chieftain's battle-scarred cheeks. He clasped Thame’s right hand between his rough, leathery palms, lowering his forehead until it rested against the young man’s knuckles in profound, wordless devotion.

Thame breathed a silent sigh of relief. He patted Kuran’s broad shoulder, stepped out into the crisp night air, and climbed to the roof of the cab once more to update the forum.

The thread had exploded into hundreds of replies from people around the globe.

Yet the newest message, pinned directly to the banner by a verified user, froze the breath in Thame’s lungs:

`@BioArch_Oxford:`
`"Everyone stop joking immediately! I enhanced the 4K raw metadata from the photo he just uploaded during that surgical incision..."`

`"Look at the necklace around the girl's throat. Those two curved canine teeth are not modern wolves. The curvature, root morphology, and serration density are a 100% anatomical match for Aenocyon dirus—the prehistoric Dire Wolf, extinct since the end of the Pleistocene ten thousand years ago!"`

`"And the ochre tattoo on her left forearm... the geometric chevron-antler motif matches the cave paintings at Lascaux and Chauvet down to the pigment grain!"`

`"You are not filming an indie movie. You are not on a soundstage in Burbank. Where on earth are you?!"`

The archaeologist’s chilling question hung unresolved on the glowing OLED screen.

Before Thame’s frozen fingers could type a syllable, Moga’s shrill, panicked shriek ripped through the darkness below.

The old shaman was standing atop the limestone boulders, pointing frantically toward the southern horizon across the valley.

Thame snapped his head up, following the old man's finger.

Far across the southern ridges and grasslands, beneath the bruised violet gloom of twilight, a monstrous pillar of oily black smoke was rising into the freezing sky.

On the shifting wind rode the sickening stench of charred raw hide, scorched fat, and burning bone.

Kuran scrambled up the iron ladder behind Thame, his jaw clenching until muscles corded against his temples. A guttural snarl of pure hatred and dread broke from the chieftain’s throat:

*"Grak... the Beast Fang Clan!"*

That dark pillar of smoke was no ritual hearth fire... it was the unmistakable signal of slaughter and raiding, creeping ever closer toward their iron bastion!
